Mufti Menk

Ismail ibn Musa Menk, is a renowned Islamic scholar and motivational speaker from Zimbabwe. He is known for his simple, relatable, and inspiring way of explaining Islamic teachings, focusing on peace, kindness, and personal development. Mufti Menk studied Shariah in Madinah and later specialized in Islamic jurisprudence in India. He travels worldwide delivering lectures that emphasize unity among Muslims and the importance of living with good character. His speeches and online presence have inspired millions of people to lead better, faith-centered lives.
